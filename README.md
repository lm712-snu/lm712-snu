## Featured projects

### 1. Mathematics of Computer Vision Language Models
**Ongoing thesis / research direction** · [CVLM thesis repository](https://github.com/lm712-snu/cvlm-thesis) · [Thesis website source](https://github.com/lm712-snu/lakshyathesis)

Studying the mathematical foundations behind computer vision and vision-language models, with an emphasis on explaining the ideas behind image processing and neural-network architectures.

**Topics:** image representations, convolution and filters, feature maps, CNN layers, activation functions, pooling, gradient-based learning, and the transition toward vision transformers.

### 2. Integrated Credit & Commodity Risk Analytics
**Quantitative analytics project** · [J.P. Morgan quantitative simulation](https://www.theforage.com/) · [Related work on GitHub](https://github.com/lm712-snu)

A portfolio of analytical tasks connecting credit scoring, loan-default prediction, expected-loss estimation, and natural-gas price forecasting.

- **FICO score quantisation:** used dynamic programming to form five monotonic risk bands from scores between 300 and 850, with reported boundaries of 521, 581, 641, and 697.
- **Loan default and expected loss:** compared Logistic Regression, Random Forest, XGBoost, and Gradient Boosting on a 10,000-record dataset; engineered debt-to-income and loan-to-income features and implemented the expected-loss relationship (EL = PD \times LGD \times EAD).
- **Natural-gas forecasting:** analysed 48 months of historical prices and explored spline interpolation alongside trend and monthly seasonal factors for forecasting.

These are simulation-project results and should be interpreted in the context of their datasets and assumptions, not as evidence of a deployed financial product.

### 3. Mathematical Foundations and Machine Learning for Chess Position Evaluation
**Mathematical modelling and ML** · [Repository](https://github.com/lm712-snu/Mathematical-Foundations-and-Machine-Learning-for-Chess-Position-Evaluation)

Explores chess through multiple mathematical lenses: formalising legal moves with logic and set theory, studying board symmetries through group-theoretic ideas, and using optimisation and supervised learning for position evaluation.

### 4. Ensemble Learning: Bagging and Random Forests
**Experimental ML study**

Compared decision trees, bagging, and random forests on the Iris dataset. The experiments examined tree depth and overfitting, bootstrap aggregation, bias–variance behaviour, robustness to feature noise and label flips, and out-of-bag error as an estimate of generalisation performance.

### 5. SMS Spam Detection
**NLP and supervised learning**

Built a text-classification workflow using a dataset of more than 5,500 messages. The pipeline included text cleaning, tokenisation, TF–IDF features, Logistic Regression, an 80/20 train–test split, and evaluation using precision, recall, F1-score, and a confusion matrix.

### 6. Contract Analyzer & Legal QA Agent

A project exploring document question answering with semantic retrieval and a LangGraph-style agent workflow. Intended capabilities include clause summaries, extraction of obligations and deadlines, structured outputs, risk-oriented review, and tool-assisted processing. The repository still has development TODOs, so the description reflects the project scope rather than claiming every capability is production-ready.
